Features at a glance

Surface areaWhat ships in the box 10 modules
WhatsApp auto‑reply A canned message or an AI reply on every incoming WhatsApp message. Embedded Signup wires customer phone numbers to your shared WABA in one click; they keep their number and their existing threads.
Risk gate A draft that quotes a price, makes a commitment, strays past the persona’s stated boundaries, or comes out a suspicious length is never sent. The customer gets a holding sentence, the tenant is emailed with the reason. Always on; nothing to configure and nothing to staff.
Human handoff The model can also ask for a person itself with [[HANDOFF]], triggered by the postamble’s universal conditions or the tenant’s own. Either way the pipeline switches to a holding‑pattern voice for the cooldown (default 60 min), then goes silent, and emails the tenant exactly once.
Persona builder A tenant‑wide AI voice from a guided form over a platform‑curated archetype (voice, business, hours, goal, catch‑phrases, off‑topics, never say, handoff conditions) or a raw prompt for power users. Every change runs through an async safety classifier before AI replies resume.
Prompt envelope Every AI reply is sandwiched by a fixed preamble + postamble (in src/prompt.rs) carrying the operating manual, jailbreak rails and the handoff sentinel. Tenant content sits in the editable middle, and all three parts are shown in the UI.
Conversation sessions Per‑customer threads carry a stable conversation_id, recent history, and any active handoff state. A tunable idle gap (default 6 h) starts a fresh conversation; a tunable cap (default 20 turns) bounds the context fed to the model.
Reply batching A Durable Object holds inbound messages for a tunable window (default 5 s) so a burst of three short messages gets one considered answer instead of three.
Interactive demo The landing page hosts a live chat where visitors roleplay as a customer of a sample business. View the prompt reveals the exact middle sent to the model. Rate‑limited per IP and globally per day. Real customer messages never arrive here.
Onboarding wizard Four steps at /wizard: business details, connect WhatsApp, choose a voice, go live. The voice stays editable afterwards from the dashboard.
Billing Prepaid reply credits via Razorpay, any quantity, no tiers. Optimistic deduction, restored on failure. Credits are granted by the webhook and by nothing else.

What was cut, and why it’s worth knowing

Earlier revisions carried Instagram DMs, a Discord relay with an approval inbox, inbound email on cncg.email subdomains with glob routing rules, a per‑channel reply‑rule engine with embedding matchers, and an approval queue. All of it was removed to reach a launchable surface. If you are reading older commits, blog posts or screenshots, that is what you are looking at.

Architecture model

Concierge runs as a single Worker serving a JSON API, plus an Elm single‑page app compiled to one static asset. State lives in Cloudflare primitives: D1 for append‑only message metadata, billing and the archetype catalog; KV for tenant config, sessions and conversation state; Workers AI for reply generation and the safety classifier; Queues for persona safety jobs; a Durable Object for the reply buffer. Email is outbound only, through the EMAIL binding, and carries nothing but handoff notifications.

Nothing stores a per‑tenant access token, so there is no secret whose rotation forces customers to reconnect — WhatsApp uses one platform token. Message content is never persisted: only metadata (channel, direction, sender, recipient, timestamp, and what was done with it).

i
What gets stored

Inbound bodies pass through the Worker and are never written to D1. Replies are generated from in‑memory data and discarded after send. Recent conversation turns do live in KV, because multi‑turn context needs them, and are cleared by the idle gap. A /data-deletion endpoint wipes the metadata; full account deletion is on the dashboard’s Settings tab.

Getting started

Self‑host: deploy the Worker, then walk the deployment guide. You’ll need a Cloudflare account (Workers, D1, KV, Queues, Durable Objects, Workers AI, Email Service), a Meta Developer app with the WhatsApp product, a Google OAuth client, and a Razorpay account if you intend to charge anyone.

bash deploy from scratch 
# 1. clone
git clone https://github.com/ananthb/concierge
cd concierge

# 2. provision Cloudflare resources
wrangler d1 create concierge
wrangler kv namespace create KV
wrangler queues create concierge-safety
wrangler queues create concierge-safety-dlq

# 3. apply migrations · D1 schema
wrangler d1 migrations apply concierge --remote

# 4. deploy · builds the Elm frontend, then the wasm
wrangler deploy

Hosted: sign in at concierge.calculon.tech, run the onboarding wizard, and you’re answering messages. The hosted service runs the same code as this repo; the difference is that we own the WhatsApp Business Account your number attaches to.
