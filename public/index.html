How it works

  • Connect your WhatsApp number

    Through Meta's own signup flow. No new number, no app to install.

  • Describe your business once

    Pick a voice, tell it what you do and when you're open. It writes the prompt for you.

  • It answers; you get the hard ones

    Anything about money or a commitment is never sent on its own — Concierge holds the conversation and emails you.
