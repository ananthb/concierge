Automated replies are either rule-matched messages you write or responses generated by replybots's built-in front-desk AI assistant. We do not guarantee that any AI-generated reply is accurate, complete, or suitable for your business. You remain responsible for all communications sent from your WhatsApp Business number.

Prepaid credits and payment

You can purchase credits anytime through Razorpay in your dashboard. A reply that fails before delivery or is withheld before sending is not charged. Purchased credits do not expire.
